Projectify Zone (“we”, “us”) is a sole proprietorship run by Shreyas P in Bengaluru, India. It provides hardware prototyping, embedded engineering and student project mentorship. These terms cover your use of this website and any work we do for you (“you” being the person or company we work with).

In short

  • Each phase has a written quote with a fixed price before it starts. You can stop after any phase.
  • Parts and fabrication are billed at cost, with receipts, and you approve the budget first.
  • On payment for a phase, you own the code, design files and documents made for you in it.
  • We keep your information confidential and sign an NDA before you share anything sensitive.
  • Prototypes are for demonstration and testing, not for sale or safety-critical use.
  • The cost estimator gives an indicative range, not a quotation.

The full terms below are what apply. If a written quote for your project says something different, the quote wins for that project.

The content on this site, including the founder guides, is general information and not professional, legal or investment advice for your situation. You may read, share and link to it; please don’t copy it wholesale or present it as your own. Links to other sites and services (such as WhatsApp and FormSubmit) are provided for convenience, and their own terms apply. How we handle your personal information is set out in our privacy policy.

2. Estimates and quotes

The prototype cost estimator and any prices on this site are indicative. They are not an offer and do not bind either of us. Before each phase we send you a written quote by email or WhatsApp that sets out the scope, deliverables, acceptance checks, price, parts budget and expected timeline. A quote is valid for 30 days. Work starts once you accept the quote in writing (a reply by email or WhatsApp is enough) and the payment due at the start of the phase is received.

3. Fees, payment and taxes

  • Phases under ₹50,000, such as a feasibility sprint, are paid in full at the start.
  • Larger phases are paid 50% at the start and 50% on delivery of that phase’s deliverables, unless the quote sets other milestones.
  • Invoices are due within 7 days. Payment is by bank transfer or UPI to the account on the invoice; we will never ask you to pay a different account by message alone.
  • Prices are exclusive of taxes. If GST applies, it is shown on the invoice at the prevailing rate.
  • If a payment is more than 14 days late, we may pause work after telling you in writing, and the timeline moves by the length of the pause.

4. Parts and fabrication

We buy components, PCBs, 3D prints and other materials for your project at cost, with receipts, within the parts budget you approve. Anything over that budget needs your approval first. Parts are paid for before they are ordered and become your property once paid for. Supplier lead times, shortages, courier delays and customs are outside our control; we tell you promptly if they affect the timeline.

5. Changes to scope

If you want something outside the agreed scope, we tell you the effect on price and timeline in writing before doing it. Nothing extra is charged without your written approval.

6. Working together

We share progress at a weekly demo or update. You agree to give us the information, decisions, feedback and access we need, normally within 5 working days of a request. If waiting on you delays the work, the timeline moves by the same amount. If we hear nothing from you for 30 days, we may close the phase and invoice for the work done so far, after telling you in writing.

7. Delivery and acceptance

At the end of each phase we deliver what the quote lists and show it working against the acceptance checks. You have 10 working days to tell us in writing about anything that doesn’t meet them, and we fix it at no extra cost. If we don’t hear from you in that time, the phase is treated as accepted.

8. Stopping or ending the work

  • Either of us can decide not to continue after any phase, with no further obligation.
  • You can end a phase early by telling us in writing. You pay for the work done up to that point (in proportion to the phase price) and for parts already ordered; we refund any advance beyond that within 14 days and hand over everything produced so far.
  • We can end the work if you don’t pay or seriously break these terms and don’t put it right within 7 days of written notice.
  • If we have to stop for our own reasons, we refund the unearned part of any advance and hand over the work done so far.

9. Intellectual property

  • Yours: your idea, information and materials stay yours. When a phase is paid in full, the deliverables we created for you in it (source code, schematics, PCB and CAD files, and documentation) are assigned to you, worldwide and for the full term of the rights. We will sign a written assignment or any other document reasonably needed to confirm this.
  • Ours: we keep the general know-how, tools and reusable code we had before your project or built independently of it. Where any of it is in your deliverables, you get a permanent, royalty-free licence to use, change and sell it as part of your product.
  • Open source and third parties: open-source software, vendor libraries and reference designs stay under their own licences. We list them in the documentation and tell you before using anything whose licence could require you to publish your own code.
  • Before payment: until a phase is paid for, you may use its deliverables for evaluation only.
  • Showing the work: we only show your project, name or logo publicly with your written permission.

10. Confidentiality

We keep what you share with us confidential and use it only for your project, whether or not an NDA has been signed; we are happy to sign yours or ours before you share anything sensitive. This does not cover information that is already public, that we already had, that we receive lawfully from someone else, or that the law requires us to disclose. The obligation lasts for 3 years after our work ends, and for as long as source code or trade secrets remain confidential. On request when the work ends, we return or delete your confidential material, except copies the law requires us to keep. During your engagement, we won’t build a directly competing product for another client.

11. Prototypes and warranty

Prototypes, proofs of concept and engineering samples are built for demonstration, testing and fundraising. They are not finished products, are not certified (for example under BIS, WPC, CE or FCC), and must not be sold or used where failure could cause injury, such as on patients or in vehicles, unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. Handle devices with mains wiring or lithium batteries as described in the documentation. Guidance we give on certification, manufacturing or unit cost is our best engineering judgement; certification testing and approvals are your responsibility unless the quote includes them.

For 30 days after a phase is accepted, we fix at no charge any defect in our firmware or design that stops the deliverables meeting the acceptance checks. This does not cover damage, misuse, changes made by others or component failures; faulty parts are handled under the supplier’s warranty. Apart from this and any rights the law gives you that cannot be excluded, the work is provided without other warranties.

Our total liability for any claim connected with a project is limited to the fees you paid us for the phase the claim relates to. Neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential loss, such as lost profits, lost funding or lost data. These limits do not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, a breach of confidentiality, or anything that cannot be limited under Indian law. Each of us is responsible for any claim that material we supplied (your specifications and content, or our original work) infringes someone else’s rights.

13. Delays beyond anyone’s control

Neither of us is responsible for delays caused by events outside reasonable control, such as natural disasters, illness, government action, or widespread component shortages. We tell each other promptly, and if a delay lasts more than 60 days, either of us may end the affected phase under section 8.

14. Founding-client offer

While founding-client slots are open, the first two projects booked get 20% off the engineering fee (not parts or fabrication) for the phases in the first quote. In return, you allow us to publish a case study after delivery. You review it before it goes live, and it contains nothing confidential. We will also ask for a testimonial: you are free to decline, and if you give one, what you say is entirely up to you. The offer depends on our capacity and can end at any time before you accept a quote; an accepted quote keeps its discount.

  • Sessions are paid for in advance. You can reschedule with at least 24 hours’ notice; a session missed without notice is counted as used.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must book and pay for the sessions and agree to these terms.

  • Messages by email or WhatsApp count as written notice and acceptance.
