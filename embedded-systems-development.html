What’s in scope

If it’s embedded, it’s in scope.

Any microcontroller, sensor, interface or protocol. Here’s what that covers in practice.

Firmware

Firmware for any microcontroller, 8-bit to 32-bit: ARM Cortex-M, ESP32 and others from NXP, ST, Espressif, Nordic and beyond.

Bare-metal & RTOS · drivers · bootloaders & OTA updates · low-power design

Electronics & PCB

Schematics, component selection and custom boards in KiCad, then bring-up on the bench.

Power circuits · sensor front-ends · design-rule checks · fab-ready files

Wired interfaces

CAN bus, Ethernet with TCP/IP, RS-485 and other industrial serial links, USB, and the on-board buses every design needs.

CAN · Ethernet & TCP/IP · RS-485 & fieldbus · USB · I²C, SPI & UART

Wireless & IoT

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular and long-range radio, talking to the cloud over the protocol that fits.

MQTT · HTTP/REST · WebSockets · cloud IoT platforms · provisioning & security

Sensors, motion & control

Clean data from sensors, motors that do what they’re told, and decisions made on the device.

Sensor selection & calibration · motor control & PID · on-device processing

Test & tooling

Test setups and desktop tools to check every board and watch the device at work.

Test jigs & scripts · desktop monitoring tools · logging & diagnostics

Who it’s for

  • You have a team and need embedded help

FAQ

Questions about embedded work.

More answers in the main FAQ and the founder guides.

Which microcontrollers and protocols do you work with?

Any. ARM Cortex-M, ESP32 and other microcontroller families; CAN bus, Ethernet and TCP/IP, MQTT, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, industrial serial links and custom protocols. If a part is a poor fit for your product, the feasibility sprint says so and suggests a better one.

Can you work on an existing board or codebase?

Yes. It starts with a short review of what exists: what’s worth keeping, what needs rework, and what it will cost. The work is then quoted as a fixed phase.

Do you only build prototypes?

The focus is getting you to a working, demo-ready device. Every investor-ready prototype includes a manufacturing roadmap and design-for-manufacture guidance, so the path to production is clear.

Who owns the firmware and design files?

You do. An NDA is signed before you share sensitive details, and on final payment you receive full ownership of the source code, design files and documentation.
