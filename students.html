Student mentorship

Project mentorship for engineering students

Alongside prototyping for founders, I mentor a small number of engineering students on their final-year and mini projects in embedded systems, IoT and robotics. The goal is simple: you build it, and you understand every part of it.

What mentorship covers

  • Choosing a topic that's achievable, relevant and interesting to evaluators
  • Planning the architecture and selecting components
  • Guidance while you write the firmware and build the hardware
  • Debugging sessions when you're stuck
  • Reviewing your report and preparing you for the demo and viva

How it works

Sessions are 1-on-1, online or in person in Bengaluru. You do the building; I guide, review and explain. That way the project, and what you learn from it, is genuinely yours.
