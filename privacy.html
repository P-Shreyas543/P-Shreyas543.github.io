Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Projectify Zone ("we", "us") provides engineering prototyping and project mentorship services from Bengaluru, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our enquiry form, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Information you give us when you fill in our project enquiry form: typically your name, contact details (phone number and/or email), your college or company, and a description of your project.
  • Information you share during a project, such as requirements documents, code or design files.

This website itself does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. It remembers your light/dark theme choice in your own browser's local storage; that setting never leaves your device.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare a proposal and quote, and to deliver the work you request.
  • To keep basic records needed for invoicing and legal obligations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your permission.

Who processes it

  • Google Forms (Google LLC) hosts our enquiry form and stores the responses. See Google's Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab).

These providers may store data on servers outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond to you and, if we work together, for the length of the project plus any period required for accounting or legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • withdraw your consent and ask us to erase it;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf.

Contact & grievances

To exercise any of these rights or raise a concern, contact us through our enquiry form (opens in a new tab) and mention "Privacy request". We aim to respond within 7 working days.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
