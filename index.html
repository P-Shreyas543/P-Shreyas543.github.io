    Questions founders ask

    Something else on your mind? Send a brief or ask on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab).

    I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

    Not at all. Most founders I work with aren't. You explain the problem and who it's for; I translate that into a technical plan and explain every decision in plain language.

    How accurate is the estimator?

    It's a realistic starting point, not a quote. It uses the effort that projects of each scope typically need, and unusual requirements can move it either way. A feasibility sprint or a short scoping call gives you a fixed price before any build starts.

    What does a hardware prototype cost?

    Feasibility sprints start at ₹15,000, proofs of concept at ₹50,000 and investor-ready prototypes at ₹1,50,000. You get a fixed quote before each phase. Components and fabrication are billed at cost with receipts.

    Full guide: what a hardware prototype costs in India

    Can you work with my chip, sensor or protocol?

    Almost certainly. The work is platform-agnostic: any microcontroller family, RTOS, sensor, display or interface, wired or wireless. If something you have in mind is a poor fit for the product, the feasibility sprint says so and suggests a better option.

    Is a prototype the same as an MVP?

    Not quite. A proof of concept shows the core idea works on the bench. A prototype looks and behaves like the product, which is what investors want to see. An MVP goes further: a small batch real customers can use. Most founders need the prototype first; it's also the starting point for MVP and product development once you've raised.

    Full guide: PoC vs prototype vs MVP

    Who owns the idea and the design?

    You do. An NDA is signed before you share sensitive details, and on final payment you receive full ownership of the source code, design files and documentation.

    Do you only work with founders in Bengaluru?

    No. Founders in Bengaluru (Bangalore) can meet in person, and founders anywhere in India can work fully online, with prototypes shipped to you.
