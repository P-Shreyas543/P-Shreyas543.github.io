How it works

A clear process, from first call to final demo.

No black boxes. You know what's being built, when, and why.

    Discovery

    Tell us your idea through the project form. We follow up to understand goals, constraints and deadlines.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    You get a scoped plan: architecture, components, milestones and a clear quote before any work begins.

  3. 03

    Build

    We develop in milestones with regular progress updates, so you can give feedback early and often.

    Handover

    Working prototype, documented source code and a walkthrough so you can explain and extend it yourself.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, complexity and timeline. After understanding your requirements we share a clear, itemised quote, so there are no surprises later.

Will I actually understand the project?

Yes. That's the point. Every engagement includes code walkthroughs and documentation, and student projects include dedicated demo and viva preparation.

How long does it take?

Small fixes can take days; full prototypes usually take a few weeks. You'll get a milestone timeline with your proposal.

Do you work remotely?

We're based in Bengaluru and happy to meet locally. Firmware, PCB, cloud and mentorship work can also be done fully online.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. On delivery you receive the full source code, design files and documentation for your project.

Ready to deploy?

Stop struggling with datasheets. Tell us what you're building, and let's build it together.
