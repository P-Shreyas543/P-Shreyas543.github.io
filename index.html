Why founders get stuck

Hardware is the hardest part of a hardware startup.

Investors want to see it work. Building it is where most early founders stall.

  • No engineering co-founder

    You know the market and the problem, but not firmware, circuits or PCB layout, and hiring a full-time engineer before funding isn't realistic.

  • Agencies built for big budgets

    Product design firms quote for corporate projects. You need an early prototype that proves the idea, scoped and priced for a startup.

  • “Show me it working”

    Slides and renders only go so far. A device on the table answers the investor's first question before it's asked.

FAQ

Questions founders ask.

Something else on your mind? Send a brief or ask on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab).

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most founders I work with aren't. You explain the problem and who it's for; I translate that into a technical plan and explain every decision in plain language.

How involved do I need to be?

About 30 minutes a week for a demo and feedback call, plus quick answers on WhatsApp when a decision needs you. Everything else, from component sourcing to firmware and PCB, is handled.

Who owns the idea and the design?

You do. An NDA is signed before you share sensitive details, and on final payment you receive full ownership of the source code, design files and documentation.

What will it cost?

Feasibility sprints start at ₹15,000, proofs of concept at ₹50,000 and investor-ready prototypes at ₹1,50,000. You get a fixed quote before each phase. Components and fabrication are billed at cost with receipts.

What if my idea isn't technically feasible?

That's exactly what the feasibility sprint is for. You find out early, for a small fixed fee, before committing a large budget. If there's a better way to achieve the same goal, you'll hear about it.

Can you work with grant milestones?

Yes. If your prototype is funded by a grant such as the Startup India Seed Fund or Karnataka ELEVATE, milestones and progress documentation can be structured to match what your incubator needs.

Do you handle manufacturing?

The focus is prototypes. Every investor-ready prototype includes a manufacturing roadmap and design-for-manufacture guidance so you know the path to production and its likely cost.

Do you only work with founders in Bengaluru?

No. Bengaluru founders can meet in person, and founders anywhere in India can work fully online, with prototypes shipped to you.
